# Baton — Screening-to-Care Coordination

Static demo of the Baton CRC pathway workspace (synthetic Abu Dhabi cohort). Open `index.html` or serve this folder.

**Start walkthrough** in the header steps through:

1. Pre-referral readiness checklist (eGFR, blood-thinner hold, ASA sedation) — referrals stay blocked until all three are cleared.
2. Async SBAR e-consult between primary care and GI.
3. Pathology hard lock on Step 10 until biopsy + MDT or 3-year recall.
4. Capacity-aware routing with effective wait and female-specialist matching.
5. Facility bottleneck radar (intake vs booking vs report review).
6. Traffic rebalancing slider (Central Digestive → Northview).
7. Policy simulator Pareto: interpreter prioritization moves language inequity from 31.4% toward 2.1% while completion still rises.

All names, MRNs, and outcome figures are synthetic demonstration data.
